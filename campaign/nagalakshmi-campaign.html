Before printing

  • Fill in the polling date and the printer's name and address in the green strip at the bottom. Election rules require the printer's details on every pamphlet.
  • On the real ballot the carrot is printed in black and white, so the sample ballot shows it that way too.
  • For about 200 voters, 300 copies on A5 paper is plenty, with spares for houses where people are away.

How to use it

  • Post it as WhatsApp status from every family member's phone, every day during the campaign.
  • Send it before each message below, so the carrot picture stays in people's minds.
  • Ask relatives in other towns to forward it to people from the village living near them.

3. Slogans

Short and plain, no big drama. Use one or two on walls and banners, and say them in conversation.

4. WhatsApp messages

Send one message every two or three days, in this order. Personal sends work better than one big forward: send to each family directly.

With only about 200 voters
  • Every vote counts. Make a list of all 200 names and mark each one: sure, maybe, or not with us.
  • She should visit every house herself at least twice. With 200 voters that takes only a few evenings.
  • On polling day, check the list by noon and personally call anyone from the "sure" list who hasn't voted yet.
  • Never offer money, liquor or gifts for votes. It's illegal, and her clean name is her biggest strength.
